# Buyer servicing

Language for the loan a buyer sees in Mellon after Substrate has funded it.

## Language

**Mellon**:
One Buyer's portal. It lists that Buyer's applications and the loans Substrate has funded for them. The sidebar is Dashboard, Active loans, and My applications.
_Avoid_: app, portfolio

**Application**:
A request for equipment financing before Substrate funds it.
_Avoid_: deal, file, lead

**Loan**:
What a funded application becomes: money the buyer is repaying to Substrate.
_Avoid_: account, contract, lease

**Balance**:
The Loan's remaining principal. Interest since the last posted installment is not part of it.
_Avoid_: payoff, amount due, remaining balance

**Buyer**:
The business signed into Mellon. They see only their own applications and loans.
_Avoid_: borrower, customer, user, client

**Dealer**:
The equipment seller named on the application.
_Avoid_: vendor, merchant

**Installment**:
The monthly amount Autopay debits. A posted one pays accrued interest first; the rest reduces Balance. A returned one changes neither.
_Avoid_: rent, bill

**Autopay**:
The ACH debit of the installment from the bank account collected at signing.
_Avoid_: payment method, mandate

**Returned payment**:
An installment debit the bank sent back, which the buyer still owes.
_Avoid_: failed payment, NSF, bounce

**Payoff quote**:
What the buyer would pay to close the loan on a chosen date.
_Avoid_: payoff letter, prepayment

**Per diem**:
One day's interest on the outstanding principal.
_Avoid_: daily rate
