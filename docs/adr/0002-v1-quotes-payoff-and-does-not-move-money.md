# v1 quotes a payoff and does not move money

Autopay already debits the installment. v1 lets the buyer see the loan, the next debit, history, and a payoff quote. It does not change the bank account, stop Autopay, or take another payment. The quote is an estimate: outstanding principal plus per diem for the actual days until the chosen date, on a 360-day year. It is not a payoff letter.

A pay button and a "request a quote" form were the alternatives. A pay button moves money this prototype does not service. A request form hides the number the buyer opened the page to see.
