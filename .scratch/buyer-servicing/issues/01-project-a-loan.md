# 01: Project a loan from its terms and ledger

**What to build:** A buyer can trust the numbers on a loan: a posted installment reduces principal by the installment minus interest, a returned payment does not, the returned debit is still the next amount due, and a payoff quote on a chosen date adds per diem through that date.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] A posted installment on the worked example leaves principal at $9,577.50
- [x] A returned installment leaves that principal unchanged
- [x] The payoff quote on the returned date is $9,644.54
- [x] That returned date is the next debit and is still due
- [x] A quote on the posted date itself adds no interest
